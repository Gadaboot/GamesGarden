Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

The short version: this app collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no data ever leaves your device.

Data we collect

None. Offline Games Arcade does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no third-party code that communicates over the internet.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data locally on your device so your games work the way you'd expect:

  • Game progress and saved games (so you can continue where you left off)
  • High scores and best times
  • Settings such as your sound preference

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Internet access

All games work fully offline and make no network requests. The only exception is the optional Support form: if you choose to send a message, the details you type (your name, email address, and message) are delivered to the developer via a form delivery service, and are used solely to reply to you. Nothing is sent unless you press Send.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future update ever changes how the app handles data (for example, adding optional online leaderboards), this policy will be updated and the change will be noted in the app's update notes before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch via the Support page.
