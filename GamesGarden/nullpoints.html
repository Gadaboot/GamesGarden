How to play

  • 1Every answer is scored 0 to 100 for how well known it is. The more obscure your pick, the less it costs you — so dig deep.
  • 2Players take turns on the same question: two answers each, four in all. You can't take one that's already gone.
  • 3Rounds come in three flavours, at random: pick from the board, type it in with nothing to choose from, or an anagram round — claim a jumbled answer, then unscramble it.
  • 4An answer we don't recognise maxes you out at 100.
  • 5Lowest combined total wins the round and takes 1 point. Level scores: drawn, nobody scores.
  • 6An answer so obscure it scores zero is a NULL — that puts £400 on the prize pot.
  • 7First to 5 points goes through to the final: two typed guesses to land a null and take the pot.
