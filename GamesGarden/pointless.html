2 Player

We asked 100 people to name as many answers as they could.
Two answers each per question — some rounds you pick from the board, some you type it in.
Lowest total takes the round. First to 5 points plays the final.

How to play

  • 1Every question was put to 100 people. Each correct answer they gave scores a point — so obscure is good.
  • 2Players take turns on the same question: two answers each, four in all. You can't take one that's already gone.
  • 3Rounds come in two flavours, at random: pick from the board, or type it in with nothing to choose from.
  • 4A wrong answer — or one that isn't on our board — stops dead on 100.
  • 5Lowest combined total wins the round and takes 1 point. Level scores: drawn, nobody scores.
  • 6An answer nobody gave is a POINTLESS ANSWER — £250 on the jackpot.
  • 7First to 5 points plays the final: three typed guesses to find a pointless answer and win the jackpot.
