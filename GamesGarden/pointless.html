How to play

  • 1Every question was put to 100 people. Each correct answer they gave scores a point — so obscure is good.
  • 2Players take it in turns on the same board: two picks each, four answers in all. You can't take an answer that's already gone.
  • 3A wrong answer stops the board dead on 100. Ouch.
  • 4Lowest combined total wins the round and takes 1 point. Level scores — the round is drawn and nobody scores.
  • 5An answer nobody gave is a POINTLESS ANSWER — it puts £250 on the jackpot.
  • 6First to 5 points goes through to the final: three guesses to land a pointless answer and win the jackpot.
