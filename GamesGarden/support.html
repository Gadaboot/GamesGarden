Frequently asked questions

Do I need an internet connection to play?
No — every game works fully offline, anywhere.

Where did my saved game go?
Each game automatically saves your progress. When you reopen a game with a save, you'll be offered "Continue" or "Start New". Choosing Start New, finishing the game, or deleting the app clears the save.

How do I turn sound off?
Tap the ☰ menu in the top-right of the main screen and toggle Sound. Some games also have their own mute button.

Will more games be added?
Yes — new games and improvements ship regularly. Use the form above to suggest one!
